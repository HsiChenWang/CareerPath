# CareerPath — “A path is made of people”

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** A career path is not a straight line or a lonely guess; it is a pattern made visible by people who have taken similar steps.
- **Identity:** The CareerPath palette: cardinal red `#990000`, deep cardinal `#6e0000`, Trojan gold `#FFCC00`, warm paper `#F7F4F0`, pale gold `#FFF4C2`, and ink `#2A1418`. Fraunces carries the emotional headline; Bricolage Grotesque carries product copy; JetBrains Mono labels the data.
- **Hero:** The supplied illustrated USC-inspired winding path, which transforms into a people network, then into a route through product moments.
- **Motion:** Graphic match cuts along the dotted path; punchy scale-through typography; radial and split reveals that turn the path into connections.
- **Arc:** Uncertainty hooks us, individual journeys become a pattern, the product makes that pattern useful, then a human connection gives it consequence.
- **Payoff:** The finale abandons interface and returns to a single route and the official supplied CareerPath mark, closing on a resolved promise that loops back into the opening dot.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape. **Runtime:** 27.0 seconds.

## Shot-by-shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–4.50s / 4.50s — “NO STRAIGHT LINE.”
**Purpose:** Meet the viewer at the moment of uncertainty, and make the path itself the unmistakable hero.

**Exact content:** At 0.00s, small mono label “CAREERPATH / USC” appears upper-left. At 0.15s, oversized Fraunces “NO” punches in. At 0.60s, “STRAIGHT” snaps beneath it; at 1.05s, “LINE.” locks in. From 1.45s, supporting line reads “A career rarely moves in one direction.” Lower-right attribution from 2.10s reads “SAMPLE JOURNEYS · NOT PROMISES”. All copy holds legibly through 3.70s before the handoff.

**Composition:** Cream paper field; supplied hero-path illustration full bleed and cropped as a 16:9 landscape plate, oversized so the serpentine path traverses the middle and right. Type owns the left third, never sitting over the route. Palette red/cream/gold. The route itself occupies ~70% of short-edge height through its bends.

**Artistic idea:** The gold dotted route escapes the illustration and becomes real graphic punctuation under the word LINE.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.15s route-dot arrives already in frame and the mono label clips on. 0.15–0.60s “NO” snaps in at a stiff scale spring; an illustration crop punches 1.06x. 0.60–1.05s “STRAIGHT” snaps on, the route dots begin tracing upward. 1.05–1.45s “LINE.” stamps down and a gold underline draws. 1.45–2.10s supporting sentence reveals in a masked line. 2.10–3.65s the illustration camera drifts 2% toward the distant campus while tiny figures remain visible; the route's next dot pulses. 3.65–4.50s that dot scales through lens, blurs and warms to gold as a branching node begins to form; headline shears out on the same beat. No empty wipe tail.

**Transition contract:** Carry the same gold dot at the route's rightmost visible bend. It expands into Shot 02's central network node while the paper field divides into cardinal-red and cream panels.

**Assets and technique:** Existing illustration `public/images/hero-path.jpg`; Fraunces / Bricolage Grotesque / JetBrains Mono; CSS/SVG route tracing, clip masks, and layered typography. No invented interface claims.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental bed, intended opening pulse and first accent; alignment unverified until generated audio is measured.
